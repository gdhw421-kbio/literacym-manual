Sign-up · App Installation · Login Guide

Step 1

Check what you need

  • Have your card or document with the coupon number ready.
  • Make sure your phone is connected to the internet (Wi-Fi or mobile data).
  • This guide is for Android phones.
LiteracyM · AndroidStep 1 / 12
Step 2

Scan the QR code or open the website

  • Scan the QR code with your phone's camera.
  • Or type the address into your browser: member.literacym.io
LiteracyM · AndroidStep 2 / 12
Step 3

Choose your language and sign-up method

  • Use the language button at the top of the screen to select the language you want to use.
  • Tap one of the Kakao, Naver, Google or Apple buttons to sign up quickly.
  • Or create a new account with your own email address and password.
  • If you sign up by email, write down the email address and password you used and keep them safe — you will need them the next time you log in.
LiteracyM · AndroidStep 3 / 12
Step 4

Accept the terms

  • Tap the “Agree to all” checkbox at the top to check every item at once.
  • Items marked “Required” in red must be checked before you can continue.
  • Once everything is checked, tap the gradient-colored button at the bottom of the screen to continue.
LiteracyM · AndroidStep 4 / 12
Step 5

Enter your details and coupon number

  • Fill in your sex, name, date of birth (YYYY-MM-DD), height (cm), weight (kg) and coupon number as shown on the screen.
  • Sex, date of birth, height and weight are used to interpret your health data, so please enter them accurately.
  • When you are finished, tap the gradient button (Done) at the bottom of the screen.
LiteracyM · AndroidStep 5 / 12
Step 6

Sign-up complete → App installation screen

  • A message confirming that your sign-up was successful appears on the screen.
  • Tap the button with the Google Play logo.
  • You are taken to the Google Play Store automatically.
LiteracyM · AndroidStep 6 / 12
Step 7

Check and install the app in the store

  • Check carefully that the name and logo are “리터러시M (LiteracyM)”.
  • Tap the “Install” button.
  • Wait until the installation finishes (this can take a while depending on your connection speed).
LiteracyM · AndroidStep 7 / 12
Step 8

Log in

  • Log in using exactly the same method you used to sign up (for example, if you signed up by email, log in by email).
  • If you signed up by email, enter the email address and password you wrote down in step 3 exactly as they are.
LiteracyM · AndroidStep 8 / 12
Step 9

Allow device notifications

  • After you log in, your phone asks “Allow LiteracyM to send you notifications?”
  • This is needed so the app can send you important information as notifications.
  • Tap the “Allow” button.
LiteracyM · AndroidStep 9 / 12
Step 10

Hospital notifications & health record sharing

  • After you log in, a section called “Hospital connection consent” may appear on the app screen.
  • Hospital connection · Health record sharing: shares your medical records with the hospital and lets you receive and view the hospital's test results.
  • Hospital notifications: lets you receive treatment information and notices sent by the hospital.
  • Check both items and tap the “Agree and connect” button.
LiteracyM · AndroidStep 10 / 12
Step 11

Logged in → Check the home screen (Done)

  • If a home screen like this appears, your sign-up, login and hospital notification consent are all complete.
  • The screen may still look empty — that is normal, because you have not added any health information yet.
LiteracyM · AndroidStep 11 / 12
Adding your health information
Step 12

Add health information (prescriptions, test results, etc.)

  • On the home screen, tap the “[Add health information +]” button.
  • Take a photo of medical documents such as prescriptions and test result sheets, or select a PDF file to upload.
  • Once uploaded, the app automatically organizes and interprets the content for you.
  • When the interpretation is finished, the app sends you a push notification.
LiteracyM · AndroidStep 12 / 12
